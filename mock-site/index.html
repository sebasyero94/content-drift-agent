Session replay

Watch how users move through your product, filter replays by the events you already track, and jump from any chart to the replays behind it.

Default replay retention: 30 days. Enterprise plans can set a custom retention policy.

Feature flags

Roll features out gradually with percentage-based rollouts and target by user properties.

Frequently asked questions

What does the Free plan include?

Up to 1M events a month, 10K session replays a month, and up to 10 active feature flags.

How long are session replays stored?

Replays are kept for 30 days by default. Enterprise plans can customize the retention period.

Do you offer startup discounts?

Yes. Eligible early-stage companies can get their first year free on the Startup plan.
